🚨 Emergency: If Customer Data Is Exposed

  1. Secure accounts — change passwords, revoke access immediately
  2. Find out what was exposed — which data, how many customers
  3. Stop the leak — remove exposed files, disable compromised accounts
  4. Notify affected customers — explain what happened and what they should do
  5. Report if required — regulators or affected individuals, depending on your location/industry
  6. Fix the cause — update policies, add 2FA, train your team
